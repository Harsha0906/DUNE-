Field manual

Your contract

Harvest 20 shards of sunglass, the glowing blue crystals that grow in open sand. Then reach the signal beacon, the orange light pillar to the south-east, and call your lift.

Stay alive

No water to worry about. You heal on your own a few seconds after a hit, and fully at condensers on the rock mesas. Supply caches hold med kits and thrum stakes. Lost your skimmer? Press R and it flies to you.

The Veil Ray

A ray the size of a cathedral swims under the dunes and hunts by vibration. Running, harvesting and the skimmer's engine all make noise. When the ground boils beneath you, sprint. A hit hurts but will not kill you outright. It cannot reach you on rock. Sandstorms muffle your footsteps.

Plant a thrum stake to lure it away.
